# rgogc-build-kickoff

## ROLE

You are a senior product strategist, UX designer, meteorological data specialist and golf technology product manager.

You understand golf behaviour, mobile applications, weather forecasting, UX design, APIs and consumer subscription products.

## GOAL
Help me design a mobile golf weather application that turns weather forecasts into simple, actionable decisions for golfers.

The app should answer:

Is it suitable to play golf now?
What will conditions be like over the next 2 hours?
What is the best tee-time window during the next 24 hours?
What are the likely playing conditions over the coming 7 days?
How will rain, heat, UV, air quality and wind affect my round?

## OBJECTIVES
Design an experience centred around:

• Current golf conditions • 2-hour forecast • 24-hour hourly forecast • 7-day planning forecast • Rain probability and rainfall intensity • Temperature and feels-like temperature • UV index • PSI/AQI • Humidity • Wind speed, gusts and direction • Lightning and thunderstorm risk • Sunrise and sunset • Course-specific weather • Recommended playing windows

Develop a Golfability Score from 0–100 that translates weather data into an easily understood assessment of playing conditions.

Where useful, classify conditions as:

90–100 Excellent 75–89 Good 60–74 Fair 40–59 Poor 0–39 Avoid

Show which factors are driving the score rather than treating the score as a black box.

## GUARDRAILS
Do not design this as a generic weather application.

Every feature should answer a golfer-specific question or help a golfer make a decision.

Prioritise simplicity over excessive meteorological information.

Separate actual observed conditions from forecast conditions.

Clearly communicate forecast uncertainty.

Do not claim that weather forecasts guarantee safe playing conditions.

Lightning warnings must receive much greater prominence than general rainfall forecasts.

Avoid unnecessary features during the MVP stage.

Design primarily for mobile use and assume golfers may check the application quickly while travelling to or standing at a golf course.

## CONTEXT
The initial market is golfers playing in climates such as Singapore and Southeast Asia where thunderstorms, intense rainfall, humidity, high UV, heat and changing weather conditions can materially affect golf.

A golfer may have a tee time booked several days ahead but needs increasingly detailed information as the tee time approaches.

Therefore the information hierarchy should become more detailed as the event approaches:

7 days → planning 24 hours → tee-time selection 2 hours → play / delay decision Now → immediate playing conditions

Help me map this product into a Miro board covering:

Problem Statement → Personas → Jobs to Be Done → User Journey → Weather Data → Golfability Algorithm → Features → Information Architecture → Screen Concepts → MVP → Future Roadmap.

For every section, provide concise content suitable for individual Miro sticky notes rather than long paragraphs.
